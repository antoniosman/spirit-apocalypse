- `node tests/engine.cjs`: passed 300 seeded playthroughs, 1,800 episodes and 23,400 decisions, including legacy save/load and invalid imports.
- `node tests/campaign.cjs`: passed 120 complete campaigns, 720 episodes and 24,657 decisions, including all player patterns, save/load, separate groups, shelters, recruitment and permanent outcomes.
- `node tests/intro.cjs`: passed exact 54–90 second planning, first credit at 65 seconds, ordered 1–3 protagonists, all 26 cast members, delayed NPC credits, immutable replay preparation and snapshot validation.
- FFprobe measured the packaged audio at exactly 90.000 seconds. The original Downloads file was not modified.
- Edge browser: a complete real-time opening entered Billy at source 65.012, Tony at 68.011 and Alex at 71.013 seconds, then the original logo reveal and episode card. Completion returned to the story.
- Replay to completion: in-memory state, localStorage save and underlying screen were byte-for-byte unchanged. Replaying episode two from a manual slot left the active episode-one story and slot unchanged. The episode card correctly displayed episode two even with no active state.
- Supporting cast layout: six visible portraits fit 390×844, 360×640, 430×932 and 844×390 viewports without horizontal overflow. Save-slot buttons fit the mobile panel.
- Offline browser test: cached audio returned HTTP 206 for a 1,024-byte range and the replay played from the requested source section without an audio error.
- Browser error console: no errors in the tested flow.

Browser tests used Edge device-sized viewports, not physical iOS/Android hardware. Older saves do not contain historical NPC casts; the replay menu explains the conservative protagonist-only fallback.
