# 1.3.0 — Clear major decisions and individual NPC credits

- Major decisions now have a prominent amber banner, warning emblem, stronger border, arrival sweep, distinct choice highlights and a two-layer sound cue. Confirmation uses the same visual treatment. Reduced-motion and sound preferences are respected.
- The sidebar displays the selected active base. Choosing a common home shows one shared base; abandoned/destroyed bases stay in save history without filling the panel. Separate active homes remain visible until a common home is chosen.
- Relationships list encountered people first and then everyone else. Each section is alphabetical, both relationship directions keep that order, and each player's knowledge remains separate.
- NPCs use the same individual cinematic portrait/name treatment as selected players. Credits adapt to cast size within the user-selected 36-second opening; no actors are omitted, including a complete 26-person cast.
- Three generated establishing shots join the intro library: railway platform, flooded underpass and radio tower. Each episode starts on a different scene, and no establishing image repeats inside the same opening.
- Music remains source 0:54–1:30 and the first player appears at 1:05. Historical cast snapshots, replay behavior and the existing title reveal remain intact.
